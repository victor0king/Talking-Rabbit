Talking Rabbitt: Resilient Conversational Business Intelligence under Real-World Schema Degradation

Published in Proceedings of the International Conference on Conversational Data Systems & Agentic AI
Abstract: Conversational business intelligence (BI) systems powered by large language models demonstrate impressive performance on pristine tabular benchmarks, yet degrade unpredictably in production where schemas suffer from syntactically abbreviated headers, mixed type formats, and semantic ambiguity. This paper introduces Talking Rabbitt, an agentic architecture designed to provide verifiable numerical outputs under schema degradation. We evaluate five representative Small and Medium Enterprise (SME) datasets across five calibrated schema corruption tiers (D0–D4). Across 750 randomized experimental trials, we identify a critical failure mode: Confident-But-Wrong (CBW) responses, which escalate from 11.1% under pristine conditions to 61.9% under compound degradation ($p < 0.001$). Our five-layer decoupled architecture mitigates this vulnerability through deterministic computation guardrails and uncertainty calibration.
